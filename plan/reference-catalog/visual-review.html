How to read the flags

"Outside the frame" means DOM bounds of text or a control. "Internal overflow" means scrollWidth exceeds clientWidth. These metrics do not catch every overlap and are not an accessibility certificate. A missing flag does not guarantee a successful composition.

Font loading and runtime errors are checked. Geometry and focus are saved in the JSON. Contrast is measured for control text with its own opaque background: results. Procedural media and transparent backgrounds need a separate check. Buttons and links in screens are demo content.
